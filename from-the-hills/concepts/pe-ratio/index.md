## What it is

P/E = share price ÷ earnings per share (EPS).

EPS is the company's profit for one year, split across all its shares. A P/E of 20 means you pay
$20 for each $1 the company earns in a year.

## How to read it

- **High P/E:** people pay a lot for each $1 of profit. They expect the profit to grow.
- **Low P/E:** cheap for each $1 of profit. People expect little growth, or see a risk.
- Compare a company with **its own past** or with similar companies. A "normal" P/E is very
  different for a bank and for a tech company.

## Example

Apple, fiscal years 2007–2026:

- **Median P/E:** 19.1.
- **End of fiscal 2026:** about 38, twice its median.
