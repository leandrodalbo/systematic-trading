## What it is

P/E = share price ÷ earnings per share (EPS).

EPS is the company's profit for one year, split across all its shares.

## How to read it

- **High P/E:** people expect profit to grow.
- **Low P/E:** people expect little growth, or see a risk.
- Compare with the company's own past, or with similar companies.

## Example

Apple, fiscal years 2007–2026: median P/E 19.1. End of fiscal 2026: about 38, twice its median.
