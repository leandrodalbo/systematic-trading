## What it is

Add up the closing prices of the last 200 trading days (about 10 months) and divide by 200. Do
it again every day, and you get a slow, smooth line under the price.

## How to read it

- **Price above the line:** a rising market.
- **Price below the line:** a falling market.
- The distance tells you how far: "−5% vs the 200-day" means the price is 5% under the line.

It's simple and slow on purpose: it ignores the noise of a few days.

## Example

On 20 November 2026, SPY closed **5.5% below** its 200-day average, after falling 11.5% from
its 6 October high. By this rule, a falling market.

- [Drops vs rises in SPY, 1993–2026](/notes/spy-drops-vs-rises/): "rising market" means price

- The daily market check: the "vs 200-day" number.
