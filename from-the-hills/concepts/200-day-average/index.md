## What it is

Add up the closing prices of the last 200 trading days (days the market is open, about 10
months) and divide by 200. Do it again every day, and you get a slow, smooth line under the
price.

## How to read it

- **Above the line:** long-term trend up, but a drop can still be going on.
- **Below the line:** long-term trend down.
- The distance tells you how far: "−5% vs the 200-day" means the price is 5% under the line.

## Example

On 20 November 2026, SPY closed **5.5% below** its 200-day average, after falling 11.5% from
its 6 October high. By this rule, the long-term trend is down.

## Where it's used

- [Drops vs rises in SPY, 1993–2026](/notes/spy-drops-vs-rises/): "rising market" means price
  above its 200-day average.
- The daily market check: the "vs 200-day" number.
