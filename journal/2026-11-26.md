- Values: trade % = profit / (entry price × shares) × 100. Ending capital = last equity. Net
  profit = ending − initial; net profit % = net / initial × 100. Exposure % = bars ending with a
  trade held / all bars. Net risk-adjusted return = net profit % / exposure %. CAR =
  (ending / initial)^(1/years) − 1 as a %, years = calendar days first to last equity date /
  365.25. MDD = largest fall from a peak in daily equity, as a positive %. CAR/MDD. Also counts:
  trades, winners, losers.

  ratios that would divide by zero are `None` (no fake numbers). A trade with exactly 0 profit is
  a loser. Every % is a percent number (5.0 = 5%).
