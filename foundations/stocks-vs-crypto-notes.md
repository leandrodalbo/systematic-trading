# Stocks vs crypto (Alpaca)

|            | Stocks                   | Crypto               |
|------------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Hours      | Mon–Fri 14:30–21:00 UK   | 24/7                 |
| Settlement | Next business day (T+1)  | Instant              |
| Protection | SIPC                     | None                 |
| Fees       | ~0                       | 0.15–0.25% per trade |
| Liquidity  | High                     | Thin nights/weekends |
| Price gaps | At the open              | None, moves anytime  |
| Shorting   | Yes (margin)             | No                   |

**So:** crypto costs more to trade, runs 24/7, and is buy-only.
