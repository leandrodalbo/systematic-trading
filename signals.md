# Signals — how you'll know it's working

Markers that separate a system you can trust from one that merely
backtested well.

- [ ] You can state your edge in one sentence, without hand-waving at "the
      market being inefficient."
- [ ] You know your strategy's worst historical drawdown before you risk a
      cent of live capital on it.
- [ ] You've watched a strategy fail out-of-sample and diagnosed exactly
      why, rather than shrugging and re-optimizing.
- [ ] Every trade has a written reason attached — the journal, not memory,
      is the record you trust.
- [ ] You'd be comfortable explaining the system to another engineer and
      defending every parameter choice.
